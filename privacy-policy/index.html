Magnetic Core · A game by Pixigo

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Magnetic Core (the “Game”) is developed and published under the Pixigo brand, operated by Aram Mehrabyan (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game on the web or a mobile device, or contact us about it. Aram Mehrabyan is the controller of personal information processed for operating the Game, except where a third-party provider acts as an independent controller.

At a glance: No account is needed to play, and progress is saved on your device. iOS 1.0.0 (build 8) has no ads. The ad-enabled 1.1.0 TestFlight beta uses test ads. The planned ad-supported App Store update will use live ads; installing the current public version does not enable them. See iOS advertising and your choices. Optional Firebase usage and diagnostics reporting starts off on a fresh install. The browser game uses Google AdSense separately. None of these versions offers real-money purchases.

Browser game at pixigo.ai/magneticcore

Browser game

This section applies to the browser game at pixigo.ai/magneticcore/. The iOS app uses separate software and privacy controls; see Advertising and consent for the differences between iOS versions.

Advertising. The browser game integrates one Google AdSense display-ad placement within its written game guide, outside the game area. The ad library is loaded when that placement comes into view or when you open advertising privacy choices. The homepage, support pages, privacy pages and error pages do not contain ad units. We request non-personalized ads. Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, approximate location, browser and device information, page URL, cookies or similar identifiers, ad views and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, prevent fraud and maintain security. Non-personalized advertising does not use your past behavior to target ads, but it can still use cookies for these other purposes.

Your choices. We use Google's consent management platform to collect applicable advertising choices. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can consent, decline or manage options in the European regulations message. Use Advertising privacy choices below the game, or Google's privacy link when available, to review or withdraw your choice. Where applicable, US visitors can use the Do not sell or share my personal information link supplied by Google. Ads and consent tools require an internet connection and may be unavailable if blocked by your browser. Declining consent does not prevent you from playing. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and non-personalized advertising.

No Firebase gameplay analytics or purchases. This web release does not load Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring or an in-app purchase SDK. We do not send scores, coin balances or skin choices as advertising parameters or gameplay analytics events. You do not need an account.

Browser storage. Scores, earned coins, unlocked cosmetics, tutorial completion and preferences are saved locally in this browser. An offline cache stores the game code, images and sounds so you can play after an initial online visit. These records are used to provide the game you requested; they are not advertising identifiers. Progress is separate from the iOS app and does not sync between browsers or devices. Clearing pixigo.ai site data removes saved progress and offline files. Private browsing, storage restrictions or browser cleanup may also remove them.

Hosting. Cloudflare delivers and protects this website. Requests for pages and files include technical information such as IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time. Cloudflare may process this information for delivery, reliability and security, and may use security cookies when its protection features require them. We use hosting to provide and protect the game, based on our legitimate interests in operating a reliable website. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and Cloudflare's cookie information.

The contact details, audience information and applicable privacy rights below also apply to the browser game. We will update this policy if these advertising practices change or we add optional gameplay analytics.

1. Information we process

Gameplay information. The Game stores progress, settings and privacy preferences on your device, as detailed below. We do not require user registration.

Gameplay analytics. When you enable Share usage & diagnostics, the native iOS Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used. Events include screen views, game modes, starts and results, scores and clears, tutorial progress, skin choices, and virtual currency earned or spent through gameplay. These events may include game and cosmetic identifiers and technical measurements. Build 8 has no advertising or real-money purchase flows. We do not send your name or email address with these events or assign an account-based user ID.

Identifiers and general location. Firebase can process app-instance or installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, language, and IP-derived general location. These identifiers can associate events with the same app installation even though you do not have an account. The build-8 iOS Firebase Analytics integration excludes advertising-identifier (IDFA) support, disables vendor-ID (IDFV) collection, and denies advertising storage, advertising user-data use, and ad personalization. App-instance or installation identifiers can still be used for optional analytics and diagnostics. The Game does not request precise device location.

Performance and diagnostics. When optional reporting is enabled, Firebase Performance Monitoring collects information about startup, screen rendering, network timing, device and app characteristics, installation identifiers, and IP addresses. We also measure gameplay actions, transitions, and visual-effect performance, including frame timing. Firebase dependencies may send SDK support diagnostics, such as log delivery and cache statistics, and platform/version information that is not linked to a user identifier. We use diagnostics to investigate reliability and improve the Game. Build 8 does not include AdMob or Crashlytics.

For details about Google's processing, see Google Analytics data disclosures, Firebase data disclosures, and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Advertising information. In ad-enabled iOS builds, Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or installation identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, crash information, performance measurements and other diagnostics. This can associate activity with a device even without a player account. See Advertising and consent. The browser game uses AdSense, not the iOS ad SDK.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include in your message. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

2. Why we use information and our legal bases

  • Deliver the Game: operate gameplay and remember settings and progress (performance of the service you request or legitimate interests, as applicable).
  • Maintain and improve it: measure gameplay and feature use, investigate technical issues, detect abuse, and improve reliability and performance (consent for optional Firebase reporting; legitimate interests where applicable to necessary security operations).
  • Deliver and measure ads: In the browser game and ad-enabled iOS builds, providers may use information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often they appear, and prevent fraud. We request non-personalized ads and seek consent where required. Non-personalized does not mean no data is processed. This does not apply to the ad-free iOS build 8.
  • Respond to requests: answer support messages and privacy requests (legitimate interests and compliance with legal obligations).
  • Meet legal requirements: comply with applicable laws and valid legal requests (legal obligation where applicable).

Current collection controls. In native iOS build 8, optional Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring reporting starts disabled on a fresh installation. Enable or disable it with Home → Settings → Share usage & diagnostics. Existing saved choices are retained when updating. Turning reporting off stops future optional reports and resets the local Analytics identifier; it does not automatically erase data already received by Google. Build 8 has no advertising consent form or advertising privacy control because AdMob and UMP are not included. Browser advertising choices are described in the web section. Some earlier TestFlight builds collected Firebase reports automatically.

3. Advertising and consent

Which iOS version do you have? App Store version 1.0.0 (build 8) and TestFlight build 9 do not include AdMob or its consent SDK. The ad-enabled 1.1.0 TestFlight beta (build 10) requests Google demo ads, not live inventory. In the planned ad-supported App Store update, the same placements will request live ads. The following practices apply to builds with ads, not to everyone who installs Magnetic Core.

When ads appear. Google AdMob may show a banner during play and an occasional full-screen ad between completed games. You can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one continuation after a loss, or complete three rewarded ads to unlock Unicorn Dreams. The game remains playable if ads are unavailable.

Your choices on iOS. The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh consent information and present a form where required. Ad requests wait until UMP indicates they are permitted. Where available, open Home → Settings → Advertising privacy choices to review or change your choices. This is separate from Share usage & diagnostics: turning off optional Firebase reporting does not itself disable ads. The game requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Browser game. Google AdSense advertising and the browser consent controls remain in use as described in the Browser game section. The native app's ad-free configuration does not disable web advertising.

Earlier native test builds. Earlier TestFlight builds included AdMob for non-personalized banners, interstitials and rewarded ads, sometimes using test inventory, with UMP consent controls where applicable. Those integrations could process IP addresses, approximate location, device identifiers, diagnostics, ad views and ad interactions. Their advertising choices were separate from optional Firebase reporting. Updating to build 8 stops those native ad requests but does not automatically erase information already received by the provider. The retention and rights sections below also apply to previously collected information.

Read Google’s iOS ad SDK data disclosures and Google’s Privacy Policy for more about provider processing and retention.

4. App Store purchases

Native iOS version 1.0.0 (build 8) and the current browser game do not offer real-money purchases or subscriptions. The paid Coin Shop is not available in build 8. Lightning Coins are earned through gameplay and can be spent on cosmetic themes and effects. Pixigo does not receive card or bank details from this release. If paid purchases are introduced in a future version, we will update these disclosures before that release.

5. Data stored on your device

Magnetic Core stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Scores and high scores
  • Lightning Coin balance
  • Unlocked and equipped skins
  • Music, sound and vibration settings
  • Privacy and optional reporting preferences

Ad-enabled iOS builds also store ad timing and reward progress locally, so completing an ad can count toward an unlock and full-screen ads are spaced apart. Consent tools may store your advertising preferences. Build 8 does not use earlier advertising records to request ads. Uninstalling generally removes local records, subject to device backups; it does not erase information already sent to a provider.

6. Service providers and sharing

On iOS, Google provides optional Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring and, in builds with ads, Google AdMob and UMP. Apple provides App Store and TestFlight distribution. For the browser game, Google provides AdSense and consent tools, and Cloudflare hosts and protects the site. See the browser section and iOS advertising section for the practices that apply to your version.

We may disclose information when required by law or to respond to valid legal requests. We do not sell personal information for money or use personalized advertising.

7. Children's privacy

Magnetic Core is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Game does not require an account, name, email address, or precise location to play. Browser advertising is requested as non-personalized. Native iOS build 8 has no advertising. The App Store age rating is a content rating and is not an age-verification mechanism.

Optional Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring are off by default in the release described above. Users must be able to provide valid consent under the law that applies to them before enabling optional collection. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

8. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here or to meet legal obligations. Game progress stored locally remains on your device until you clear it, uninstall the Game, or the operating system removes it, subject to any platform backup settings. Our providers may apply their own retention periods. Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and related obligations.

Analytics retention depends on the settings for our Google Analytics project and Google's applicable policies. Firebase states that Performance Monitoring keeps IP-associated events for 30 days and installation-associated and de-identified performance data for 60 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. See Firebase's retention information. Contact us for information about applicable retention settings or to request deletion of information that can be associated with you.

We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.
