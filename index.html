More than fitting shapes

Plan the placement. Change the board.

Place & clear

Fit pieces onto an 8 × 8 board. Complete a row or column to clear it. Work through all three pieces in your hand, keeping space for the next set.

Push & pull

Positive cores push nearby blocks away; negative cores pull them closer. Use the preview to fill a gap or open room for a larger shape.

Build a finish

Consecutive clears build a combo. Earn a Finisher Core to clear three horizontal rows, including granite that ordinary magnets cannot move.

Two ways to find your rhythm

Classic gives you time to think. Study the empty spaces and compare moves without a countdown. Flux Rush begins with two minutes and adds three seconds for each completed line. It rewards recognizing a good placement quickly, while still leaving room to play.

Both modes award Lightning Coins through completed runs. The Cosmetic Lab lets you preview and unlock themes, from glowing circuits and underwater effects to rainbow unicorns and a collapsing singularity. The browser game has no real-money purchases.

Made for a quick break, with room to learn

Use the in-game tutorial to practise the special pieces. Adjust music, sound and placement assistance in Settings. After the first online download is complete, you can install the game to your home screen and play offline.

Your scores, earned coins and themes are saved locally in the browser you use. They do not require an account or sync to other devices. Clearing browser data can remove them; our support page explains storage, offline play and common fixes.
