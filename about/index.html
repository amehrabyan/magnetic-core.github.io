The people behind the game

About Pixigo

Pixigo is an independent app publishing brand operated by Aram Mehrabyan in Spain. Magnetic Core is our original single-player block puzzle. We develop the game, maintain this website and write the guides that explain its rules.

Why Magnetic Core?

Fitting a shape into an empty space is easy to understand. Magnetic Core adds a second decision: where will the existing blocks move after the piece lands? Push and pull can turn a blocked area into an opening, or undo a plan if you ignore the preview. Classic gives that decision breathing room; Flux Rush puts it against a clock.

We keep the rules consistent across cosmetic themes. The Lab changes the colors, music and effects, while the board still depends on placement, lines and magnetic movement. Lightning Coins earned through play give players a way to work toward a different look.

What you can use here

The browser game is playable without creating an account. The field guide explains the current rules and offers practical placement advice. The support page covers progress, installation, audio and reporting problems.

The browser version may show a labeled AdSense display ad within its guide. Ads do not change the rules or unlock rewards. The current native iOS build 8 is ad-free. Our privacy policy distinguishes these versions and describes local storage and optional native reporting.
