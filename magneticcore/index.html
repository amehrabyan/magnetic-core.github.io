The Magnetic Core field guide · By Pixigo

Make room for your next move.

Magnetic Core is an original single-player puzzle game built around an 8 × 8 board. You place block shapes, clear complete rows and columns, and use magnetic cores to move blocks that are already on the board. The challenge is to keep useful space open while building a score. You can play the full browser game above for free, without an account or a download.

Your first run

  1. Start with Classic. Choose Classic on the home screen and press Play. It has no timer, so you can examine a placement before committing. The in-game How to Play guide includes practice for special pieces.
  2. Look at the whole hand. You receive three shapes. Every square of a shape must fit inside the board on empty cells. Place all three to receive the next hand. A small piece can be useful for filling a gap, but using it first may block a larger shape.
  3. Preview, then place. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board and release at a valid position. If dragging is uncomfortable, look in Settings for placement assistance. The placement preview helps you see where the piece will land and what its magnet will do.
  4. Complete a line. Fill all eight cells in any row or column to clear that line and make space. Scattered groups of the same color do not clear by themselves. Keep checking the empty spaces as the board changes.

A run can end when none of the remaining pieces fits. During the opening warm-up the game is more forgiving; later, keeping a useful shape of empty space matters as much as the amount of empty space.

Use the board you already have

Blue + cores push

A positive core pushes nearby movable blocks away from it. A push can open a pocket for your next shape or move a block into a nearly complete line. Check the preview: an occupied destination or the board edge can prevent a move.

Red − cores pull

A negative core pulls nearby movable blocks toward it. Use a pull to bring a block into a gap, but watch the space you leave behind. The blocks in the piece you just placed stay in place while its magnetic effect moves existing blocks.

A useful planning habit: before dropping a magnetic piece, identify one gap you want to fill and one space you need to preserve. Compare the preview with both goals. A line clear is valuable, but a move that leaves room for the rest of your hand can keep the run alive longer.

Granite blocks resist magnetic movement. They can still be removed by completing their row or column, or by placing a Finisher Core whose highlighted band covers them.

Choose your pace

Classic

There is no countdown. Take time to compare placements and learn how push and pull affect the board. Try to preserve both a long lane and a wider opening so different shapes have somewhere to go.

Flux Rush

You start with two minutes. Each completed line adds three seconds. Efficient clears extend the run, but a full board can still stop you before the timer does. Learn the shapes in Classic, then use that familiarity to make quicker decisions here.

The pause control lets you adjust settings or leave a run. Returning to the home screen ends that run; it is not a cloud save or a way to resume the same board on another device.

Build a streak, earn a Finisher

Clearing on consecutive placements builds a combo and raises your score multiplier. A placement without a clear breaks the streak. Magnetic clears also charge the Overdrive meter. Ordinary line progress and a full Overdrive meter are two ways to earn the same special piece: a gold Finisher Core.

Your first Finisher is earned after three completed lines; later ones require eight. When earned, it arrives with a new hand. Place it on a valid empty cell to clear the highlighted band of three horizontal rows, including granite inside that band. At the board edge, the band shifts inward so it still covers three rows. It does not erase arbitrary neighboring blocks or clear the entire board.

Check the band before placing. A Finisher can rescue a crowded area, but spending it on an almost empty band wastes much of its space-clearing value. Only genuinely completed lines add time in Flux Rush; blasting extra blocks is not a substitute for completing lines.

Earn a different look

Completed runs award Lightning Coins. Spend them in the Cosmetic Lab to unlock visual themes and effects. Preview a theme before choosing: the collection includes circuit-inspired Ion Drift, Bioluminescent Abyssal, Unicorn Dreams, Glitch Protocol and Singularity Void. Themes change the presentation, not the basic placement and line-clearing rules.

The browser game has no real-money purchases and no rewarded-ad unlocks. Scores, earned coins, unlocked themes and settings stay in this browser. They are separate from the iPhone app and do not sync between devices.

Common questions

Can I play offline?

After opening the game online, wait for “Ready for offline play” below the board. The downloaded game can then run without a connection. On iPhone or iPad, Safari's Share menu offers Add to Home Screen; on supported desktop and Android browsers, use Install. Advertising and online support pages require a connection.

Why did my progress disappear?

Progress is local to the browser and device you used. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or moving to another browser can remove or hide it. If browser storage is blocked, the game warns that progress lasts only for that session. A board in progress is not restored after a page reload.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers may wait for your first tap before allowing audio. Start a game, check your device volume, then check Music and Sound Effects in the game's Settings. A muted tab or device can silence the game even when those switches are enabled.

Where do ads appear?

When available, the browser version has one labeled display-ad space within this guide, away from the board and game controls. Ads do not award coins, change the rules or unlock themes. Blocking an ad or declining optional advertising consent does not prevent play. Native iOS build 8 is ad-free.

How can I report a problem?

Visit Magnetic Core support for troubleshooting and contact details. Include your browser, device and the steps that caused the problem. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
